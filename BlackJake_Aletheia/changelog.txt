# Version 0.9.0.1 # : ??/??/2025
    == New features ==
        - The Bank has been added to the game!
            * via the bank menu, you can now Deposit and Withdraw cash between rounds
            * via the bank menu, you can now Take out a Loan at leisure, reinforcing your gambling addiction in exchange for a small percentage of your balance money after each round!
            * you can also choose to repay the loan by the amount you want between rounds
            * be careful; if you don't have enough cash to repay the loan, it's game over!
    == Changes ==
        - any amount of money in an input that exceeds the maximum available value will now be considered the maximum amount of money to be returned (i.e.: Cash = 100, Bet = 0 | bet 999 -> Cash = 0, Bet = 100)
        - the command system has been reworked and now accepts multiple arguments in a single line (i.e, you can now do "bet 100 play" while before you had to do "bet 100" and then "play")
